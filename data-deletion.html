Uninstalling is not cloud deletion.

Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local app data, but does not by itself remove your Firebase account or synced settings. Use guest deletion or request account deletion below to remove cloud data too.

01 / Before you start

This guide covers App Blocker Pro by Sahed Alom Sumit, Android package com.sahed.app_blocker_pro. Usage history stays on your device; Google and online guest accounts can have settings stored in Firebase.

Finish active focus sessions and turn off local protection before resetting or using in-app deletion. Save anything you need to keep. Account and data deletion is permanent; deleted guest accounts cannot be recovered. Guest accounts are also not recoverable after uninstalling unless previously linked to Google.

02 / Request account and cloud-data deletion

For a Google-linked account, or if you cannot access the app, send a deletion request to sahedalomsumit@gmail.com. You do not need to reinstall the app to make a request.

  1. Use the subject “App Blocker Pro account deletion”.State that you want your App Blocker Pro account and associated cloud settings deleted.
  2. Identify the account.For Google sign-in, email from the Google address linked to the app, or include that address. For a guest, include an account identifier if you still have one. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes.
  3. Complete any ownership verification.We may ask for the minimum information needed to confirm ownership. We will confirm the scope and status by email. A lost anonymous account may not be identifiable without its account identifier.

Requests are handled as soon as reasonably possible and within applicable legal deadlines. If verification or additional time is needed, we will explain by email. This page opens your email app; it does not submit a request automatically.

03 / Delete guest data from the app

If you are using an anonymous or offline guest, the app includes a dedicated deletion flow:

  1. Connect to the internet if a cloud guest account exists.Finish focus sessions and disable local protection first.
  2. Open Settings → Account.Choose “Manage account and guest data,” then “Delete guest data.”
  3. Read the warning and choose “Delete permanently.”The app pauses uploads, deletes cloud settings and the anonymous Firebase account, then clears all local app data, including usage history. The app closes and starts fresh next time.

If deletion fails or shows as incomplete, settings sync stays paused to prevent re-upload. Reconnect and retry Delete guest data. Do not assume a failed attempt removed everything.

If the guest option is not available because you use Google sign-in, use the email request option above. Signing out is not account deletion: it keeps local settings and continues as a guest.

04 / Clear local history and settings

To remove app-managed information from this device:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → App Blocker Pro.You can also long-press the app icon and select App info.
  2. Open Storage or Storage & cache.Choose “Clear storage” or “Clear data” and confirm. Labels vary by Android version. Clearing only the cache does not remove all settings and usage history.
  3. Or uninstall the app.Choose Uninstall from the app’s Android settings. If Android offers to keep app data, leave that option unchecked for a full local removal.

Local clearing removes stored rules, preferences, usage records, and app-managed local logs. It does not remotely erase another device. Cloud settings may return if you sign in again before deleting the account’s cloud data.

05 / Delete backups and copies separately

The current app allows Android app-data backup. Review your device’s Android/Google backup settings and use the provider’s controls to manage backed-up app data. Restoring a device backup may restore previously saved local information.

If you exported a settings backup, a log, or another file, delete it from Files, Downloads, your chosen folder, and any cloud storage or trash folder. Clearing app storage and deleting an account do not delete exported files, email attachments, or copies shared to other services. Those copies are governed by the service or recipient holding them.

06 / What is deleted and what may remain

  • Account deletion: removes the App Blocker Pro Firebase authentication account and its associated live cloud settings/reducer configurations after ownership is verified or the in-app guest flow succeeds.
  • Local app reset: removes app-managed usage history, rules, preferences, and other private local data on that device. In-app guest deletion performs this reset after completing required cloud deletion.
  • Not deleted automatically: app data on other devices, exported files, device/provider backups, your Google account, and information held independently by Google Play or other services.
  • Limited retained records: support correspondence, deletion-verification records, or information required for legal or security obligations may be retained only as necessary. Provider-managed backup copies may persist according to the provider’s retention practices rather than being erased immediately.

There is no server-side usage-history copy in the settings-sync system to delete. We cannot remotely erase history stored only on your device. If you want to remove a specific support email or attachment you sent us, include that in your request.

07 / Need a hand?

Email sahedalomsumit@gmail.com with your account type, the action you tried, and any error message. Never include protection passwords, authentication tokens, sign-in codes, or private browsing history.
